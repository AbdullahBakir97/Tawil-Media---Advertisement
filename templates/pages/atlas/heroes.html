The chosen hero is the mix below, and it is the one the home page ships. The five single-idea proposals it was mixed from follow, kept for reference. Everything is built with the system's own tokens, fonts and motion, at real size. Hover, resize, switch to Arabic and to dark mode.

{# ---------- D · Broadsheet ---------- #}

D · Broadsheet typographic masthead, dateline, three-column front page, category marquee

No hero image at all: the type is the image. Feels like a serious newspaper front page and works with any content. Best for an editorial-first identity; least suited to promotion.

How to choose
A

Needs a strong cover photo each edition. Most "magazine".

B

Safest all-rounder; brand and product together.

C

Warm publisher feel; shows depth of archive.

D

Editorial authority; no imagery needed.

E

Atmosphere and pitch; needs great imagery or video.
